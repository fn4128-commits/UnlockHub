**【提交文本 / EN】**
```
UnlockHub is a personal utility hub built around how you actually use your
phone. It brings together three things people juggle across separate apps:
a low-friction daily check-in for family, reminders you will not miss, and
simple automations triggered by everyday phone events.

Daily check-in (for family peace of mind)
- Records only the first screen unlock of each day and shares it with a
  contact you choose, so loved ones can see you are up and about without
  anyone sending a message.
- 72-hour inactivity alert: if no new check-in arrives for three days, an
  alert is created for your contact.
- Weekly summary of activity for your contact.
- A cloud monitor also checks for missing check-ins periodically, so alerts
  do not depend on the recorder phone staying online.

Memos and reminders
- Quick memos with scheduled reminders.
- Optional sync so your notes are backed up and available to you.

Automations (routines)
- Trigger an action when something happens: a set time, your first unlock
  of the day, plugging in the charger, low battery, connecting to a Wi-Fi
  or Bluetooth device, or arriving at a place you choose.
- Actions include toggling Do Not Disturb and other quick device tasks.

Permissions and data
- The check-in and memo features sync data (an install ID, a display name,
  the contact handle you enter, unlock timestamps, and memo content) to the
  UnlockHub backend so features work across devices and reach your contact.
- Optional location and Bluetooth permissions power the place/connection
  routine triggers and can be left disabled.
- Data is sent over HTTPS. UnlockHub shows no ads and has no in-app
  purchases.
- Full privacy policy: TODO(用户：填入托管后的隐私政策 URL)

Contact
- Email: TODO(用户：填入对外支持邮箱)

UnlockHub is an early-stage app. Please use a nickname or handle for your
contact while testing rather than a full phone number.
```
